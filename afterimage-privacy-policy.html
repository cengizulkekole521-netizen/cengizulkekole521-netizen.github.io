Afterimage — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how XOXANA GAMES™ ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Afterimage (the "Game"). By installing or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data we do not collect

Afterimage does not require an account, login, or any personal information to play. There are no user profiles, no chat features, and no server-side storage of your identity. Your progress — completed rooms, stars, achievements, unlocked characters, and settings — is saved only on your own device using local storage, and is never transmitted to us.

2. Data collected by third-party services

The Game uses the following third-party services, which may independently collect limited technical data as described in their own policies:

Advertising — Google AdMob

Afterimage shows optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and device/usage data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases — Google Play Billing

Character unlocks and the "Remove Ads" option are purchased through Google Play's billing system. Payment information is handled entirely by Google Play and is never seen or stored by us. See Google Play's Terms: play.google.com/about/play-terms.

3. Children's privacy

Afterimage is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

4. Data retention and deletion

Since all gameplay data lives locally on your device, uninstalling the Game or clearing its app storage permanently deletes your saved progress. We hold no copy of it.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game evolves (for example, if new features or services are added). Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
